# Open Challenges

- **1. Data Accessibility & Quality:**
  - A major bottleneck for effective ML-based detection is the lack of large, reliably labeled datasets of illicit activity.
  - On-chain data is vast and noisy, off-chain data (from CEXs) is siloed and restricted, and cross-chain data is often incomplete or entirely missing.

- **2. Pseudonymity and Decentralization:**
  - Privacy-Enhancing Technologies (PETs) like mixers and AECs are explicitly designed to thwart analysis.
  - The decentralized and autonomous nature of some protocols makes them resistant to traditional sanctions and takedowns, as seen in court rulings regarding Tornado Cash.

- **3. Regulatory Arbitrage:**
  - The borderless nature of Web3 allows launderers to easily move funds to and operate through VASPs based in jurisdictions with lax or non-existent AML enforcement, undermining global efforts.
