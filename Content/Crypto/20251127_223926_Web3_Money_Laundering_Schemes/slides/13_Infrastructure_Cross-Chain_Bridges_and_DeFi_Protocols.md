# Toolkit: Bridges and DeFi Protocols

### Cross-Chain Bridges
- **Function:** Protocols that enable the transfer of assets and data between otherwise isolated blockchains.
- **Laundering Use (Layering):**
  - **Chain-hopping:** Bridges are a key enabler of this strategy. They sever direct provenance links by "locking" an asset on a source chain and "minting" a new, wrapped equivalent on the destination chain.
  - **Impact:** This makes tracing the flow of illicit assets significantly more difficult for investigators.

### DeFi Protocols
- **Function:** A suite of financial primitives like lending platforms and yield aggregators.
- **Laundering Uses (Layering):**
  - **Lending:** Pledge illicit crypto as collateral to borrow clean funds.
  - **Yield Farming:** Invest tainted assets to earn "clean" rewards, commingling funds in the process.
  - **Liquidity Pools:** Create fake tokens and pair them with legitimate assets to drain clean liquidity from a DEX.
