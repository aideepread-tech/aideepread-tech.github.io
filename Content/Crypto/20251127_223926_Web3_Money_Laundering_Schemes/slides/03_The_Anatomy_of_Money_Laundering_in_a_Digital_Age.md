# The Anatomy of Money Laundering in a Digital Age

- **Core Definition:** The process of concealing the illicit origins of criminal proceeds to make them appear legitimate.

- **Ultimate Goal:** To integrate unlawful gains into the legal economy, allowing criminals to use their profits without suspicion while hiding the underlying crimes.

- **Why Web3 is a Target:**
  - **Pseudonymity:** Transactions are tied to addresses, not real-world identities, obscuring the flow of funds.
  - **Decentralization:** The lack of central intermediaries hampers traditional regulatory and law enforcement efforts.

- **Crypto-Native Crime:** Laundering is not just for external criminal proceeds; it is a critical step for cashing out profits from Web3-native crimes like hacks and scams.
