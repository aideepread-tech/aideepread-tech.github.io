# Taxonomy Part 2: The Launderer's Intermediary Toolkit

High-level strategies are put into practice using a variety of specific intermediary mechanisms. These are the purpose-built tools and repurposed infrastructures that facilitate the laundering process.

This toolkit can be broken down into two categories:

1.  **Privacy-Enhancing Technologies (PETs):** Tools explicitly designed to provide anonymity.
    - *Examples: Mixing Services, Anonymity-Enhanced Coins*

2.  **Repurposed Infrastructure & Instruments:** Legitimate Web3 services and financial instruments that are exploited for illicit purposes.
    - *Examples: Exchanges, DeFi Protocols, NFTs, Gambling Platforms*
