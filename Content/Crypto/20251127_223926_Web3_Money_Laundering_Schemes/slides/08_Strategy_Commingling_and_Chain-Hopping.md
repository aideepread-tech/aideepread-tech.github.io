# Strategies: Commingling & Chain-Hopping

### Commingling
- **Goal:** To obscure the origin of illicit funds by mixing them with a larger volume of legitimate assets.
- **Method:** Illicit and clean funds are pooled within a shared transactional or custodial structure (e.g., an exchange's deposit wallet or a mixing service).
- **Effect:** This shifts anonymity from simple pseudonymity to **set anonymity**, where the launderer is just one participant in a large group, making direct attribution difficult.

### Chain-hopping
- **Goal:** To disrupt traceability by moving assets across multiple, distinct blockchain networks.
- **Method:** Using cross-chain bridges, decentralized exchanges (DEXs), or centralized exchanges (CEXs) to convert an asset on one chain (e.g., Ethereum) to an asset on another (e.g., Solana).
- **Challenge for Investigators:** This forces forensics teams to aggregate and correlate data from entirely separate, heterogeneous ledgers.
