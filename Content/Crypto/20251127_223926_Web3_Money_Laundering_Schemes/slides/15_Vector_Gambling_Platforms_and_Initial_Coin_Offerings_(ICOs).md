# Toolkit: Gambling and ICOs

### Online Gambling Platforms
- **Vulnerability:** Many crypto-native gambling sites operate with minimal regulation and no AML controls.
- **Laundering Method:**
  1.  **Placement:** Deposit illicit funds into a gambling account.
  2.  **Layering:** Place numerous small bets (TFO) and mix funds with legitimate winnings (Commingling). A common tactic is "chip dumping"—intentionally losing to a co-conspirator in a poker game.
  3.  **Integration:** Withdraw the balance, now disguised as legitimate gambling proceeds.

### Initial Coin Offerings (ICOs)
- **Vulnerability:** A fundraising mechanism with a history of regulatory gaps and fraud.
- **Laundering Method:** Invest illicit crypto into an ICO, commingling it with funds from legitimate investors. The new tokens received in return appear as standard investment gains. Launderers can even create a counterfeit ICO to control the entire flow.
