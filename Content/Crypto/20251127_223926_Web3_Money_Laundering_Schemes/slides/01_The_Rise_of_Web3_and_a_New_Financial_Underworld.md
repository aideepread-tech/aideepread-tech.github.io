# The Rise of Web3 and a New Financial Underworld

- **Web3 & Decentralized Finance (DeFi):** An ecosystem built on smart contracts and blockchain for borderless, trustless, and permissionless financial services.

- **The Paradox of Innovation:** While enabling unprecedented access to financial tools, Web3's nature presents substantial regulatory challenges.

- **A Fertile Ground for Crime:** The absence of centralized oversight and technical complexity create opportunities for financial crimes, particularly money laundering.

- **The Core Problem:** Illicit gains from scams, code exploits, market manipulation, and trafficking are covertly moved and legitimized through the Web3 ecosystem.
