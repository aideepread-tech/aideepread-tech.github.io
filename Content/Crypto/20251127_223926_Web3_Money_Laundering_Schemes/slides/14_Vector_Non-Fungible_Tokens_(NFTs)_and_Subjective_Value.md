# Toolkit: Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs)

**Function:** Unique digital certificates of ownership, whose value is often highly subjective and opaque.

**A Vector for All Laundering Stages:**
- **Placement:** Illicit fiat can be converted to crypto by purchasing an NFT in a P2P transaction.

- **Layering:** The core strategy of **Exploiting Subjective Valuation** is applied here. Launderers use collusive wash trading—selling an NFT back and forth between controlled wallets at escalating prices—to create the appearance of legitimate, high-value art sales.

- **Integration:** The now "valuable" NFT can be sold to an unsuspecting buyer or a colluding party to reintroduce the cleaned funds into the economy.

- **Metaverse Connection:** Virtual worlds like Decentraland, where NFT trading is rampant and often lacks KYC, are fertile ground for this activity.
