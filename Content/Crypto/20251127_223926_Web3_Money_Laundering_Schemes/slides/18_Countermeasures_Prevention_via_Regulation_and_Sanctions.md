# Countermeasures: Prevention

**Goal:** To deter and obstruct illicit activity by enhancing transparency, establishing controls, and enforcing consequences.

- **Regulatory Deterrents:**
  - Global bodies like the FATF have extended AML guidelines to **Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs)**—exchanges, wallet providers, etc.
  - This mandates **Know Your Customer (KYC)**, transaction monitoring, and reporting of suspicious activity.

- **Blocklisting and Sanctions:**
  - Identifying and "tainting" addresses and coins associated with financial crime.
  - The U.S. Treasury's OFAC has famously sanctioned mixers like Tornado Cash for their role in laundering.

- **Deanonymization and Prosecution:**
  - Law enforcement and blockchain forensic firms work to unmask illicit actors, enabling legal investigation and prosecution.
