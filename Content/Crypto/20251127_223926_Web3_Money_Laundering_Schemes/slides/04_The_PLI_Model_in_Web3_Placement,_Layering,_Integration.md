# The PLI Model in Web3: Placement, Layering, Integration

The traditional three-stage model of money laundering, adapted for the crypto ecosystem:

- **1. Placement:**
  - **Goal:** Introduce illicit funds into the financial system.
  - **Web3 Method:** Converting illicit fiat currency into crypto assets, often using loosely regulated centralized exchanges (CEXs) to evade detection.

- **2. Layering:**
  - **Goal:** Conceal the origin of funds through a series of complex transactions.
  - **Web3 Method:** Employing obfuscation strategies like mixers, chain-hopping, and complex transaction flows to break the financial trail.

- **3. Integration:**
  - **Goal:** Reintroduce laundered funds into the legitimate economy.
  - **Web3 Method:** "Off-ramping" crypto back into fiat or purchasing high-value assets like real estate, luxury goods, or NFTs.
