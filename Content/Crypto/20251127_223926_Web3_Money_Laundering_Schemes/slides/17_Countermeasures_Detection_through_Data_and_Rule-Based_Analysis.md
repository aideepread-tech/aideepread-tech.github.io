# Countermeasures: Detection

**Goal:** To uncover and flag suspicious laundering activity by monitoring and analyzing transactional data.

### Rule-Based Detection
- **How it Works:** Uses predefined rules and thresholds crafted by experts (e.g., flag transactions over $10,000, or patterns resembling smurfing).
- **Pros:** Interpretable and easy to align with specific regulations.
- **Cons:** Prone to high false positives, rules are static and easy for criminals to circumvent, requires constant manual updates.

### Data-Driven Detection (Machine Learning)
- **How it Works:** Employs algorithms to analyze complex patterns in transaction graphs, classifying illicit entities or detecting anomalous behavior.
- **Pros:** Can adapt to new and evolving laundering tactics, reduces reliance on manual rule creation.
- **Cons:** Heavily dependent on the quality and quantity of labeled training data, which is scarce.
