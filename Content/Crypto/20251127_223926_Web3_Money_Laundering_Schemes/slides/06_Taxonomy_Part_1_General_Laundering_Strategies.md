# Taxonomy Part 1: General Laundering Strategies

Laundering schemes rely on a core set of high-level tactics to obscure the origin, flow, or ownership of illicit assets. Our taxonomy groups these into six broad categories:

1.  **Transaction Flow Obfuscation (TFO):** Deliberately complicating transaction patterns.

2.  **Commingling:** Mixing illicit funds with assets from multiple legitimate sources.

3.  **Chain-hopping:** Moving assets across different blockchain networks to disrupt traceability.

4.  **Disconnected Fund Flow (DFF):** Using techniques that eliminate any traceable on-chain link.

5.  **Exploiting Subjective Valuations:** Laundering through assets with no fixed market price, like NFTs.

6.  **Exploiting Regulatory Gaps:** Leveraging inconsistent AML enforcement across jurisdictions.
