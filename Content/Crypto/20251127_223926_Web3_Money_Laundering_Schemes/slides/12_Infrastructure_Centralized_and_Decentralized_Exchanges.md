# Toolkit: Cryptocurrency Exchanges (CEX & DEX)

### Centralized Exchanges (CEXs)
- **Role:** A pivotal gateway between the fiat and crypto worlds, making them exploitable at all three laundering stages.
- **Laundering Uses:**
  - **Placement:** Converting illicit fiat to crypto in jurisdictions with no KYC.
  - **Layering:** Using internal, off-chain transfers and chain-hopping (depositing on one blockchain, withdrawing on another).
  - **Integration:** Serving as the primary "off-ramp" to convert crypto back to fiat.

### Decentralized Exchanges (DEXs)
- **Role:** Autonomous, non-custodial trading platforms powered by smart contracts.
- **Laundering Use (Layering):**
  - **Transaction Flow Obfuscation:** Swapping tainted assets across various liquidity pools.
  - **Chain-hopping:** Executing atomic cross-chain swaps to move assets between networks.
