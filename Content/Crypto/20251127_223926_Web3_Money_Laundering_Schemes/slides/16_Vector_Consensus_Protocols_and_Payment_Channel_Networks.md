# Toolkit: Consensus & Payment Channels

### Consensus Protocols (PoW & PoS)
- **Function:** The mechanisms that secure a blockchain.
- **Laundering Uses:**
  - **Placement (PoW):** Invest illicit fiat into mining hardware and electricity. The newly mined, "clean" coins have no prior transaction history, breaking the laundering trail (a form of DFF).
  - **Layering (PoW/PoS):** Use hash rental services or stake illicit funds to generate fresh, untainted assets as rewards. Liquid staking derivatives further complicate tracing.

### Payment Channel Networks (PCNs)
- **Function:** Off-chain scaling solutions like the Lightning Network, designed for fast, low-cost payments.
- **Laundering Use (Layering):** Intermediate transactions within a payment channel are not recorded on-chain. This allows launderers to fragment and route illicit funds through multiple off-chain paths, completely obscuring the trail (DFF).
