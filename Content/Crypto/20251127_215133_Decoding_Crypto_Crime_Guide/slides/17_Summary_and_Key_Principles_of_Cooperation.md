# Summary & Key Principles

*   **Crypto is Traceable:** The immutability of the blockchain creates a permanent evidence trail. Don't assume funds are lost forever.

*   **Time is Your Most Critical Asset:** Act immediately to intercept funds before they are confirmed on the blockchain.

*   **Evidence is Everything:** Methodically collect wallet addresses, transaction hashes, and all communications.

*   **Collaboration is Non-Negotiable:** Engage with VASPs, international partners like INTERPOL and Europol, and internal experts.

*   **Leverage Your Tools:** Use free wallet explorers for initial checks and commercial analytics software for deep investigations.

*   **Protect the Victim:** Provide immediate support and warn them about the high risk of secondary scams.
