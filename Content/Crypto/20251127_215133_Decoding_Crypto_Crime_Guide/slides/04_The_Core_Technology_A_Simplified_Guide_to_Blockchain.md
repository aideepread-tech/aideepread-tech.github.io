# The Core Technology: A Simplified Guide to Blockchain

*   **What is it?** A decentralized, distributed digital ledger (DLT).

*   **Key Features:**
    *   **Decentralized:** No single person or entity is in control. Changes require majority consensus.
    *   **Immutable & Transparent:** Transactions are recorded in chronological "blocks." The entire chain is visible to all participants, and past blocks are nearly impossible to alter.
    *   **Resilient:** With no central point of failure, the network can operate even if some users ("nodes") are attacked.

*   **Analogy:** Think of it as a shared Google Sheet where all changes are tracked, visible to everyone, and cannot be secretly deleted.
