# The Crypto Ecosystem: Exchanges, Mixers, and VASPs

*   **Exchanges:** Platforms for trading cryptocurrencies.
    *   **P2P (Peer-to-Peer):** Users trade directly with each other.
    *   **CEX (Centralized Exchange):** A company facilitates trades (e.g., Binance, Coinbase).
    *   **DEX (Decentralized Exchange):** Automated, smart-contract based trading.

*   **Mixers and Tumblers:**
    *   Services that obscure the trail of transactions by mixing funds from many users. A key tool for money laundering, but tracing is still possible via "demixing" services.

*   **VASPs (Virtual Asset Service Providers):**
    *   The official FATF term for any entity providing crypto services (exchanges, wallets, etc.). Often required to comply with AML/CTF regulations. The EU uses the similar term **CASP** (Crypto Asset Service Provider).
