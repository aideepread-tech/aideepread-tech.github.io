# The First Four Questions: Crucial Information to Collect

When a victim reports a crypto crime, every second counts. Prioritize collecting these four vital pieces of information:

1.  **Time:** When did the transaction happen? If very recent, there's a chance to stop it.

2.  **Financial Institution:** Which bank or VASP (crypto exchange) was used? This is the point of contact for intervention.

3.  **Size:** What was the transaction amount? Large transfers often trigger AML compliance checks, creating a window to act.

4.  **Type of Cryptocurrency:** Which asset was involved? Traceability varies significantly between coins like Bitcoin (traceable) and Monero (difficult).
