# Crypto Infrastructure: Wallets, Addresses, & Explorers

*   **Crypto Wallets:**
    *   **Function:** Where cryptocurrency is stored.
    *   **Forms:** Can be software (apps), hardware (USB-like devices), or even paper.
    *   **Recovery:** Most have a 12- to 24-word recovery phrase to restore access.

*   **Wallet Addresses:**
    *   **Function:** Like a bank account number for sending and receiving crypto.
    *   **Format:** A long, case-sensitive string of letters and numbers (e.g., `bc1qu5z7kn0v2krhglsnan4c0m5f76xk69p53wjwgh`).

*   **Wallet Explorers:**
    *   **Function:** A search engine for the blockchain (e.g., Etherscan, Blockchain.com).
    *   **Use:** Allows anyone to view transactions, balances, and history of a public wallet address.
