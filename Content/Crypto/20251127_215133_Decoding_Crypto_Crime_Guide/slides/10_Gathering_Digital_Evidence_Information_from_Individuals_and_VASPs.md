# Gathering Digital Evidence

**From the Victim (Individual):**
*   **Crucial:** Wallet addresses (victim and suspect) and Transaction IDs/Hashes.
*   **Context:** All communications with the scammer (emails, chats, social media).
*   **Records:** Bank statements, promotional materials, and details of any software they were asked to install.

**From the VASP (via Law Enforcement Request - LER):**
*   **Identity:** Name, DOB, copies of ID documents used for account creation.
*   **Activity:** Transaction logs, timestamps, IP addresses, device info, and customer service notes.

**Pro Tip:** Always request transaction data from VASPs in `.csv` format for easier analysis.
