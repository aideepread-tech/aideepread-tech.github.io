# Investigative Tools: Blockchain Analytics

**1. Wallet Explorers (Free Tools)**
*   **What they are:** Public search engines for a specific blockchain (e.g., Etherscan for Ethereum, Blockchain.com for Bitcoin).
*   **What they show:** Transaction history, wallet balances, fees, and sending/receiving addresses.
*   **Use Case:** Quick, initial analysis of a suspect wallet.

**2. Blockchain Analytics Providers (Commercial Software)**
*   **What they do:** Specialized software to monitor, visualize, and analyze blockchain activity at scale.
*   **Advanced Capabilities:**
    *   Track funds across different cryptocurrencies.
    *   Visually map complex transaction flows.
    *   Assign risk scores to wallets.
    *   "Demixing" services to trace funds through privacy-enhancing mixers.
