# Common Crime Types: Investment & 'Rug Pull' Scams

**Cryptocurrency Investment Schemes**
*   **The Lure:** Scammers, often posing as expert investors, promise lucrative returns.
*   **The Hook:** They ask for a small upfront fee (€250-€1000) to start. To build trust, they might show fake account gains or even send a tiny "payout."
*   **The Disappearance:** Once a significant amount is invested, the scammer vanishes.

**'Rug Pull' (or Exit) Scams**
*   **The Setup:** Developers create hype around a new cryptocurrency project or NFT collection, attracting many investors.
*   **The Pull:** After collecting enough money, the developers abandon the project, drain the funds, and disappear. The digital asset becomes worthless overnight.
