# Common Crime Types: Extortion, Sextortion, & Phishing

**Extortion & Sextortion**
*   **The Tactic:** A scammer sends an email claiming to have hacked the victim's device and recorded compromising videos (e.g., sextortion). They demand crypto payment to prevent release.
*   **The "Proof":** They often include an old password of the victim, found in a public data breach, to seem credible.

**Phishing Scams**
*   **The Goal:** To steal a victim's private keys (the password to their crypto).
*   **The Method:** Scammers create fake websites mimicking legitimate exchanges or wallets. When the victim logs in, their credentials are stolen.
*   **Variant - "Address Poisoning":** A scammer sends a tiny transaction from a wallet address that looks nearly identical to one the victim frequently uses, hoping for a mistaken future payment.
