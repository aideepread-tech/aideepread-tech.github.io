# Initial Response Protocol: Time is Critical

Cryptocurrency transactions are irreversible once confirmed. The primary goal is to act *before* the transaction is finalized on the blockchain.

**Immediate Actions:**

1.  **Stop the FIAT Transfer:** If a bank transfer was just initiated (especially on a weekend or evening), instruct the victim to contact their bank immediately to stop the payment.

2.  **Contact the VASP/Exchange:** If funds reached an exchange, have the victim (or LEA) contact the platform's fraud/compliance department with urgency. Use email subjects like **"URGENT: FRAUD - STOP TRANSACTION"**. Many large VASPs have dedicated Law Enforcement Request systems.

3.  **Engage International Channels:** For cross-border transfers, LEAs can use **INTERPOL's I-GRIP** (Global Rapid Intervention of Payment) tool to request a payment stop in the destination country.
