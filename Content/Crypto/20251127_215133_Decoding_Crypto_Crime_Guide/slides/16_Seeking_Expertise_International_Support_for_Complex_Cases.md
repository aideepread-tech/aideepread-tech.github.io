# Seeking Expertise: International & Local Support

No agency has to work in isolation. Leverage these resources:

*   **Local Expertise:** Identify and build connections with experienced officers within your own agency's crypto or cybercrime units.

*   **Europol (for EU members & partners):**
    *   **Europol Platform for Experts (EPE):** A free, secure network for LEAs to share knowledge, best practices, and access webinars.

*   **INTERPOL:**
    *   **IFCACC:** Assists with cross-border fraud, anti-money laundering, and asset recovery.
    *   **I-GRIP:** A rapid intervention channel to request international payment stops.

*   **Other Key Partners:**
    *   **UNODC:** Offers basic and advanced training workshops.
    *   **Basel Institute on Governance:** Provides in-depth training on crypto investigations.
