# From Investigation to Court: Preparing the Case

**1. Investigative Stage: Follow the Digital Trail**
*   Use blockchain analysis tools to trace the flow of assets across wallets and platforms.
*   Identify the use of anonymizing services like mixers or privacy coins.
*   Collect digital evidence: IP addresses, transaction timestamps, and crypto wallet data.

**2. Trial Preparation: Make it Understandable**
*   **Simplify Complexity:** Explain blockchain and cryptocurrency concepts in clear, non-technical language. Visual aids are highly effective.
*   **Present a Clear Narrative:** Show a concise digital trail of the illicit funds from source to destination.
*   **Connect Virtual to Physical:** Link the movement of crypto to the purchase of real-world assets or other criminal activities.
