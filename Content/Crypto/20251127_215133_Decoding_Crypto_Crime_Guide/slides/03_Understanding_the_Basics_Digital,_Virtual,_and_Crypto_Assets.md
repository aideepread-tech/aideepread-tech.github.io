# Understanding the Basics: Asset Types

### A Hierarchy of Digital Value

1.  **Digital Assets (Broadest Term)**
    *   Any asset in digital form: images, documents, and all virtual currencies.

2.  **Virtual Assets**
    *   A digital representation of value that can be digitally traded or transferred.

3.  **Crypto Assets (Technology-Specific)**
    *   A virtual asset built on a Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT), like a blockchain.

4.  **Cryptocurrencies (Use-Specific)**
    *   A crypto asset designed to be used for payment, trade, or transfer (e.g., Bitcoin, Ethereum).
