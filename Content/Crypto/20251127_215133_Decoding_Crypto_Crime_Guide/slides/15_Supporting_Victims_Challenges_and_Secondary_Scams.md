# Supporting Victims: Beware of Secondary Scams

Victims of a crypto scam are often targeted again. Warn them about these common follow-up frauds:

*   **The "Saviour" Deception:** A new scammer contacts the victim, posing as a recovery expert who can get their money back for an upfront fee. They take the fee and disappear.

*   **The "Whistleblower" Ruse:** The scammer pretends to be a repentant ex-employee of the fraudulent company, offering inside help to reclaim assets... for a fee.

*   **The Mirage of Legal Recourse:** Fake lawyers charge victims high rates for drafting generic, useless legal documents, providing false hope while draining more funds.
