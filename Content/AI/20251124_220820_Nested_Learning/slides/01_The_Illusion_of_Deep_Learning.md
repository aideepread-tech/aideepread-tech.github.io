# Nested Learning: The Illusion of Deep Learning Architectures

**Authors:** Ali Behrouz, Meisam Razaviyayn, Peiling Zhong, Vahab Mirrokni (Google Research)

---

**Core Argument:**

Despite decades of progress, deep learning models face fundamental challenges in continual learning and adaptation. The conventional approach of stacking layers provides capacity but not necessarily greater computational depth or the ability to acquire new knowledge efficiently.

This paper introduces **Nested Learning (NL)**, a new paradigm that reframes models not as flat architectures, but as a system of nested, multi-level optimization problems, each with its own "context flow."
