# Discussion: The Future of Continuous Learning

The ACE framework has important implications for the future of AI.

- **Alternative to Fine-Tuning:** Adapting contexts is a flexible and efficient alternative to retraining/fine-tuning models for continuous learning and handling distribution shifts.
- **Enables Selective Unlearning:** Because contexts are human-interpretable and modular, specific pieces of outdated or incorrect information can be precisely removed without affecting the entire system.

ACE provides a practical path toward more responsible and continuously evolving AI systems.
