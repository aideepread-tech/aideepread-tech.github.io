# Evaluation: Testing ACE on Demanding Benchmarks

ACE was evaluated on two categories of applications where comprehensive, evolving contexts are most critical:

1.  **LLM Agent Benchmarks (AppWorld):**
    - Requires multi-turn reasoning, tool use, and environment interaction.
    - Agents can accumulate and reuse strategies across episodes.

2.  **Domain-Specific Benchmarks (Financial Analysis):**
    - Tasks (FiNER, Formula) demand mastery of specialized concepts and tactics (e.g., XBRL).
    - Success depends on a deep, explicit knowledge base.
