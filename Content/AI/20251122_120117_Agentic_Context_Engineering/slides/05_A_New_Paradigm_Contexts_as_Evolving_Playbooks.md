# A New Paradigm: Contexts as Evolving Playbooks

To overcome these limitations, we must shift our perspective. Instead of treating contexts as concise summaries, we should view them as **comprehensive, evolving playbooks**.

- **Old Idea:** Compress knowledge into short prompts.
- **New Idea:** Saturate the context with abundant, detailed, and rich domain insights.

LLMs are highly effective when provided with long, detailed contexts. The goal is to **preserve** domain-specific heuristics and tactics, allowing the model to distill relevance autonomously at inference time.
