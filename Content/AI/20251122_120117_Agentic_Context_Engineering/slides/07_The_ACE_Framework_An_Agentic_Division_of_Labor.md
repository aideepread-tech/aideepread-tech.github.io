# The ACE Framework: An Agentic Division of Labor

ACE employs a modular, agentic architecture with three specialized roles, mirroring a human learning process:

1.  **The Generator:**
    - Executes tasks and produces reasoning trajectories, surfacing successes and failures.

2.  **The Reflector:**
    - Critiques the Generator's traces to distill concrete, actionable insights and lessons.

3.  **The Curator:**
    - Integrates these insights into the context through structured, incremental updates.
