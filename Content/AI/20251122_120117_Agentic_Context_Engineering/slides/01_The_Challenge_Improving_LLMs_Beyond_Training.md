# The Challenge: Improving LLMs Beyond Training

Modern AI systems increasingly depend on enhancing Large Language Models (LLMs) *after* their initial training is complete. Instead of costly and slow model weight updates, the focus has shifted to **context adaptation**—modifying the model's inputs to improve performance on specific tasks.

This approach underpins critical components like:
- System prompts that guide behavior
- Memory that carries past experiences
- Factual evidence to reduce hallucination

However, existing methods for context adaptation face fundamental limitations.
