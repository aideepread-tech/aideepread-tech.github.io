# From Visual Prompts to Concept Prompts

**The Evolution of SAM:**

*   **SAM 1 & 2 (Promptable Visual Segmentation - PVS):**
    *   Focused on segmenting a **single object** per prompt.
    *   Used **visual prompts**: points, boxes, or masks.
    *   Achieved a breakthrough but didn't address finding *all* instances of a concept.

*   **SAM 3 (Promptable Concept Segmentation - PCS):**
    *   Introduces **concept prompts**: text phrases (e.g., "all cats") or image exemplars.
    *   **Finds and segments all instances** of a concept in an image or video.
    *   Represents a paradigm shift from local visual cues to global conceptual understanding.
