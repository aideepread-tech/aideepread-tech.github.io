# Performance: State-of-the-Art in Video PCS

**SAM 3 also establishes a new SOTA for open-vocabulary video instance segmentation.**

*   **SA-Co/VEval Benchmark:**
    *   Largely outperforms baselines like GLEE and systems using other detectors (LLMDet).
    *   Achieves over **80% of human performance** on the pHOTA metric.
    *   Excels in both frame-level detection (pDetA) and cross-frame association (pAssA).

*   **Public Benchmarks:**
    *   Achieves new SOTA results on **LVVIS** (36.3 mAP) and **OVIS** (60.5 mAP).
    *   Shows strong performance on BURST, YTVIS21, and others.
