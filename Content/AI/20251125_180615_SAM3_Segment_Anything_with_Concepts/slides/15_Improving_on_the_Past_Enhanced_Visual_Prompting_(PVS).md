# Improving on the Past: Enhanced Visual Prompting (PVS)

In addition to the new PCS task, SAM 3 also improves upon the core visual prompting capabilities of SAM 1 & 2.

*   **Video Object Segmentation (VOS):**
    *   SAM 3 achieves significant improvements over SAM 2 on most benchmarks.
    *   On the challenging **MOSEv2** dataset, SAM 3 outperforms prior work by **6.5 points** (60.3 vs 53.8 J&F).

*   **Interactive Image Segmentation:**
    *   Evaluated on the extensive 37-dataset benchmark.
    *   SAM 3 outperforms SAM 2.1 in segmentation accuracy (mIoU) with 3 or more clicks.
