# SAM 3: Segment Anything with Concepts

**Nicolas Carion, Laura Gustafson, Yuan-Ting Hu, et al.**

*Meta Superintelligence Labs*

---

**Abstract:** We present Segment Anything Model (SAM) 3, a unified model that detects, segments, and tracks objects in images and videos based on **concept prompts** (e.g., noun phrases, image exemplars). SAM 3 doubles the accuracy of existing systems in both image and video Promptable Concept Segmentation (PCS) and improves upon previous SAM capabilities. We open source SAM 3 and the new **Segment Anything with Concepts (SA-Co)** benchmark.
