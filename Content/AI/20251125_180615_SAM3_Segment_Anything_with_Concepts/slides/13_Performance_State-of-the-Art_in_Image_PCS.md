# Performance: State-of-the-Art in Image PCS

**SAM 3 sets a new state-of-the-art in zero-shot image concept segmentation.**

*   **SA-Co/Gold Benchmark:**
    *   Achieves a `cgF1` score of **54.1**, more than **double** the strongest baseline (OWLv2* at 24.6).
    *   Reaches **74%** of estimated human-level performance.

*   **LVIS Benchmark:**
    *   Significantly outperforms prior best on mask AP (**48.5** vs. 38.5 from DINO-X).

*   **Semantic Segmentation:**
    *   Outperforms strong specialist baselines on datasets like ADE-847 and Cityscapes.
